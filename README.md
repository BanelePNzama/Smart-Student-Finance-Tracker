Smart Student Finance Tracker
- A simple and intuitive tool for students to manage their finances. Track income and expenses, analyze spending habits, and generate reports to make smarter financial decisions.

Features
- Add Income & Expenses – Easily record bursaries, allowances, or any income, and track all spending.

- Categorize Expenses – Organize expenses by categories like Food, Transport, Books, etc.

- Auto Calculate Balance – Instantly see remaining budget after each transaction.

- Reports & Charts – Visualize spending patterns to understand where your money goes.

- Budget Warnings – Get alerts if you exceed your planned budget.

Tech Stack
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript.

- Data Storage: browser localStorage (Data saved in JSON format)

- Charts: Chart.js
